# Economic Review Skill

Purpose:
Generate structured economic review reports linking macroeconomic events
to sector impacts and bank customer risks.

Core Logic

Macroeconomic Event
→ Transmission Mechanism
→ Sector Impact
→ Supply Chain Impact
→ Customer Portfolio Exposure
→ Credit Risk Implication

Output Structure

1 Situation Overview  
2 Transmission Mechanism  
3 Sector-Level Impact  
4 Supply Chain Impact  
5 Customer Portfolio Exposure  
6 Credit Risk Implications  
7 Opportunities

Writing Style

Clear  
Structured  
Executive-level briefing  
Avoid academic jargon
